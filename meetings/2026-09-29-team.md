| ID | Decision | Rationale | Alternatives rejected |
|---|---|---|---|
| D-20260929-01 | Use **PostgreSQL**, with JSONB columns, for the shared-context store | The data is relational — claims, evidence, symbols, dependents and alerts all reference each other.
